## Publishing source

**Practical DevSecOps** (individual certifications and certification bundles). The vendor's own article "Best InfoSec Black Friday Deals 2026" (published 3 November 2026) lists per-certification sale prices at 15% off. The vendor's banner cards state the same prices in USD, so currency is recorded as USD only where the banner matches the article card. The site simultaneously labels the discount a "CSA Month Sale" and shows expiry only as a countdown; both facts appear in each offer's conditions and no end date is invented. Bundle cards publish from their own original and discount price fields, and only when the vendor's stated saving equals the difference; as of 8 November all fourteen live bundles pass that check. Bundles that include a "Coming Soon" course (three, all containing CAASE) stay candidates, as does the CAASE course itself. Bundle currency is left unspecified because the USD banner cards cover single certifications only.
