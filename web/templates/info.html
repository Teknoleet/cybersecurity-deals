A 2026 date is the first filter, not the last.

An offer must explicitly belong to a 2026 campaign, or be supported by an article originally published in October 2026. A footer year, a course title containing a year, or an updated 2025 roundup is not enough. A dated discovery article still needs matching current seller evidence for its particular product and offer.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday offers are labeled separately from general promotions. Offers with unresolved dates, conflicting prices, historical codes, or unsupported evidence are withheld. Future offers are labeled upcoming. Known expired or withdrawn offers leave the current directory.

Published does not mean checkout-tested.

Vendor-published means an official source supports the offer. Marketplace-published means an allowed third-party seller supports it. We do not complete checkout, test coupon redemption, guarantee stock, or endorse the quality of a listed product.

Relevant third-party book and training bundles can be included, with the seller identified. Software-license offers are excluded unless seller authorization can be established. General AI productivity products do not qualify merely because they use AI; cybersecurity and AI security do, including privacy and VPN products.

Freshness is shown, not assumed.

The Last Check card records the latest completed collection cycle. It does not mean every source was successfully checked. Each offer has its own confirmation time, separate from the source fetch and collection timestamps.

After three hours without confirmation, an offer is marked Recheck pending. Fetch failures retain last-known-good data without changing its confirmation time. A blocked page does not prove a sale has ended. Warnings are derived in your browser, so they still appear if collection stops.

Advertised savings are not historical proof.

Prices remain in the source's original currency. If a symbol does not establish which currency applies, we say so rather than assuming. Missing prices, expiry dates, VAT information, renewal terms, or time zones are not invented.

Crossed-out prices are vendor reference prices. Any observations collected here only cover the time since tracking began and must match the same product, plan, billing period, currency and scope. They do not establish a prior market low.

A small, transparent collection pipeline.

Source configuration, category mappings and corrections are managed through Git. Public pages and supported feeds are checked with bounded requests; private communities, logins, access restrictions and CAPTCHAs are not bypassed. There are no visitor accounts, submissions, paid placement, or purchase flows.

The site is a directory, not a seller. Always confirm the final price, coupon, access duration, regional eligibility, taxes and renewal terms with the seller before buying.
